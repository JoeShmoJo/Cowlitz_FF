BACKGROUND
----------------------------------------------------------------
The regulated flow at a given AEP carries uncertainty from two
independent sources: the unregulated frequency estimate itself (HEC-SSP
confidence limits), and the scatter of the unregulated-to-regulated
transform about its fitted line. EM 1110-2-1619 (29 Sep 2025) Sec.
4-4.b(3), Eq. 4-6, combines independent standard deviations by
root-sum-of-squares; Sec. 4-6a treats each side of the best estimate as
its own Normal distribution, which is how the asymmetry in both the SSP
limits and the transform residuals survives the combination instead of
being averaged into one symmetric spread.

The reviewer's formula for the combination itself:

  Upper = RegBest + sqrt((Unreg97.5 - UnregBest)^2 + (Transform97.5 - TransformBest)^2)
  Lower = RegBest - sqrt((UnregBest - Unreg2.5)^2 + (TransformBest - Transform2.5)^2)

at the 95% two-sided level (z = 1.960). The transform term is
unambiguous: the LOESS fit's own confidence spread, evaluated at the
unregulated best estimate, already in regulated cfs. The frequency term
is where the two approaches below diverge.

THE TWO APPROACHES
----------------------------------------------------------------
literal  (FREQ_TERM_MODE = "literal")
    freq_term = Unreg_bound - Unreg_best, taken directly in
    UNREGULATED cfs and combined as-is against the transform term.
    This is the reviewer's formula exactly, read literally: the
    formula as given subtracts unregulated flows and adds the result
    to a regulated-cfs quantity. It does not push the unregulated
    bound through the transform first.

transform_curve  (FREQ_TERM_MODE = "transform_curve")
    freq_term = Transform(Unreg_bound) - Transform(Unreg_best), i.e.
    the unregulated bound is pushed through the SAME fitted transform
    curve as the central estimate before differencing, so the result
    is already in REGULATED cfs -- the same units as the transform
    term it is then combined with.

WHY THEY DIFFER
----------------------------------------------------------------
"literal" adds an unregulated-cfs delta to a regulated-cfs quantity
without converting units first. That is only an approximation of the
true regulated-cfs delta -- the exact conversion is Transform(Unreg +
delta) - Transform(Unreg), which is what "transform_curve" computes,
and the two are equal only where the transform is locally linear.

The transform is NOT linear -- its local slope (d log(reg) / d log(unreg))
runs about 0.6 through the middle of the curve, where the projects
absorb most of an increase, and rises toward 1.5 near the top, where
the curve bends toward 1:1 pass-through. Where the slope is below 1,
"literal" OVERSTATES the frequency term relative to "transform_curve"
(an unregulated-cfs delta is larger than the regulated-cfs delta the
project would actually produce from it); where the slope approaches or
exceeds 1, near pass-through, the two converge and can cross.

Neither is "more correct" about the AEP or the best-estimate curve --
both use the same reg_curve and the same transform term. They differ
only in how the frequency term is expressed before combining, i.e. how
literally the reviewer's formula is followed versus how dimensionally
consistent the combination is.
