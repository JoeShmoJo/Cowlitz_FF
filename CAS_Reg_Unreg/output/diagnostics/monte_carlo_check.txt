WHAT THIS CHECKS
----------------------------------------------------------------
The adopted band combines the frequency-term sigma and the
transform-term sigma by root-sum-of-squares on each side separately
(the closed-form two-piece-lognormal treatment). That combination
rule is exact for two ordinary Normals, but only an approximation
once the two things being combined are themselves asymmetric -- the
true combined distribution of two independent split-Normals is not
itself exactly a split-Normal. This check draws directly from the
two source distributions (50000 draws per AEP, split-Normal on each
side using the same sigmas already adopted), pushes the frequency
draws through the actual fitted transform curve, and compares the
simulated 2.5/97.5 percentiles to the closed-form band.

RESULT
----------------------------------------------------------------
The closed-form band is close to the simulated band but not
exact -- the largest difference at any AEP is 11.8%, at the
most asymmetric AEPs. Small enough to note rather than act on,
but the closed-form band is an approximation, not the final word,
at the most lopsided AEPs.
